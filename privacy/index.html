Sketch FEA Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Summary

  • Your sketches, projects, pictures, and results stay on your device; the app never uploads them. We do not receive them.
  • The app has no account and no ads, and it does not track you.
  • Apple handles all payments. We never see your name, email address, or payment details from a purchase.
  • To check Sketch FEA Pro, the app uses a subscription service provider. It receives an anonymous ID, a device identifier, and your purchase history, but not your name or contact details (section 2).
  • If you email us, we receive your email address and what you send.

This policy explains what information the Sketch FEA app for iPhone and iPad collects, why, and what choices you have. Sketch FEA is made by Omnis Dragon LLC, doing business as Journey On (“we”, “us”), 116 Agnes Rd, Ste 200, Knoxville, Tennessee 37919-6306, United States. We are responsible for the information described here; in European and UK law we are the “controller”. Questions: support@journeyon.co.

1. Information that stays on your device

  • Sketches, projects, custom materials, and settings are saved in the app’s storage on your device. If you back up your device to iCloud or a computer, they are included in that backup, which Apple’s or your computer’s terms govern. Deleting the app deletes them from the device. We do not receive them.
  • Calculations run on your device. No calculation runs on a server.
  • Pictures. When you choose a background image to trace over, the app opens only the photo or picture you choose or take. It is read on your device and is not uploaded. It is kept on your device and is not part of a saved sketch file.
  • Camera and photo access. The app asks iOS for access only when you choose to take or pick a picture. You can change this at any time in the iOS Settings app, under Sketch FEA.
  • Files you export. “Save as a file”, “Copy report”, and “Copy” put a file or text where you choose, for example Files, Mail, AirDrop, or another app. The service you choose handles it under its own terms. We do not receive it unless you send it to us.

We do not send your sketches, pictures, or results to any other company.

2. Subscriptions

Purchases go through Apple. Apple processes payments and manages your Apple Account under Apple’s Privacy Policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). Apple does not give us your name, email address, or payment details.

To check Sketch FEA Pro, the app uses a subscription service provider. The app makes this check when it is online, whether or not you subscribe. The provider receives:

  • An anonymous ID that the app creates. It contains nothing about you. The app shows it in Menu > About Sketch FEA as “Purchase ID”.
  • A device identifier: the random ID that iOS gives to our apps on your device.
  • Your Sketch FEA purchase history from the App Store, such as the plan, the dates, and the price.
  • The technical details that come with any internet request, such as the iOS version and the IP address.

We use this information only to provide Sketch FEA Pro, to prevent fraud, and to count trials and subscriptions. It is not linked to your identity, and it is not used for advertising or tracking. The provider processes it only for us and protects it as this policy does. It is kept as a purchase record, stored in the United States. To have it deleted, see section 9.

3. Support emails

Email support (Menu > About Sketch FEA) opens a draft in your Mail app. Below a line it lists the app version, device model, iOS version, Sketch FEA Pro status, and purchase ID. You can change or delete any of this before you send it. When you email us, we receive your email address, any name your email includes, and what you send. We use it only to answer you. We keep support emails for 24 months after the last message, then delete them. Our email is hosted by Google (Google Workspace).

4. Information Apple may give us

If you allow it in iOS Settings (Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements > Share With App Developers), Apple may give us crash reports and usage statistics for the app. App Store Connect also gives us totals such as downloads and sales. Apple provides these under its own privacy policy, and they do not identify you to us.

5. Our website

The pages at sketchfea.journeyon.co are hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which may log the IP address of visitors for security. The pages use no cookies and no analytics.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal information, and we do not “share” it for cross-context behavioral advertising, as California law defines these terms.
  • We do not show ads, use advertising identifiers, work with data brokers, or track you as Apple defines tracking.
  • Because we do not track you, the app has no setting for “Do Not Track” signals.

7. Legal bases (European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland)

  • The subscription check: to perform our contract with you (providing Sketch FEA Pro), and our legitimate interests in preventing fraud and knowing how many subscriptions we have.
  • Support emails: to answer your request.
  • Camera and photos: your permission in iOS. The pictures are processed only on your device.

8. How long information is kept

  • On your device: until you delete it or delete the app. Copies in device backups stay until those backups are deleted.
  • Purchase record (section 2): as long as we offer Sketch FEA Pro, unless you ask us to delete it sooner (section 9).
  • Support emails: as in section 3.
  • Apple’s records: under Apple’s privacy policy.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Use the app offline. The free features work without a connection, and the app makes no subscription check while it is offline. Sketch FEA Pro needs at least one online check.
  • Camera and photo access. Change it at any time in the iOS Settings app.
  • Delete your purchase record. Email support@journeyon.co with the purchase ID from Menu > About Sketch FEA. We will have the record deleted within 30 days and confirm by email. Deleting the record does not cancel a subscription; cancel it in Settings > your name > Subscriptions. If the app is still installed, its next online check sends the information again, so delete the app before you ask.
  • Your rights. Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, to restrict or object to its use, and to withdraw consent. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, you can also complain to your data protection authority. Residents of California and other U.S. states with privacy laws have similar rights, and we will not treat you differently for using them. To use a right, email support@journeyon.co. We answer within the time the law sets. To find your purchase record we need the purchase ID; we will not ask for more information than we need.

10. Children

Sketch FEA is rated 4+ on the App Store because it contains no objectionable content. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The only information that leaves the device without the user sending it is the subscription check in section 2, which contains no name or contact details. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child under 13 has sent us personal information, for example by email, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Security

Information on your device is protected by iOS. The subscription check uses encrypted HTTPS connections. No system is completely secure.

12. International users

We are based in the United States, and the purchase record is stored in the United States. Transfers from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland are made under standard contractual clauses. You can ask us for a copy at support@journeyon.co.

13. Changes to this policy

We post changes on this page with a new “Last updated” date. If a change affects what the app collects, we say so in the app’s update notes before it takes effect, and we update the App Store privacy information.
